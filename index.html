TVHS Cross Country 2026

TVHS held its place in the Southwestern League this season, with the boys third and the girls fifth at league finals, but the real story is improvement underneath those finishes. The girls' scoring five averaged about 2½ minutes per runner faster than last year, 20 of 24 returning athletes ran faster at league finals on the same course, and freshmen gained the most over the season, an average of 1:52 off their opener. This page covers 64 athletes across four meets through November 5: each athlete's season improvement, comparisons with 2025, how the scoring pack ran together, and team results.
